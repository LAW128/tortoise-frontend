Explore our growing library of conservation resources, research publications, educational materials, and community toolkits designed to support amphibian, reptile, and tortoise conservation across Ghana and beyond.

At Tortoise People Project, we believe that knowledge is one of the most powerful tools for conservation. Our resources are developed in collaboration with scientists, community leaders, and conservation practitioners to ensure they are accurate, practical, and accessible to everyone — from school children learning about wildlife for the first time to policy makers shaping the future of environmental protection in West Africa.

Whether you're a researcher looking for data on Ghana's tortoise populations, a teacher seeking classroom materials on biodiversity, or a community organiser interested in starting a local conservation initiative, our resource library is here to support your work. We regularly update our collection with new findings, guides, and case studies from the field.
