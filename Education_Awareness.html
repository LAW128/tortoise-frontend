Education Awareness Program

The Tortoise and People Project’s Education and Awareness Program connects people with nature by helping communities understand the importance of tortoises and their ecosystems. Through storytelling, school programs, community outreach, and hands-on learning experiences, we inspire people of all ages to become active guardians of wildlife and their natural habitats.

We believe communities are the strongest defenders of nature. By creating awareness and building connections between people and tortoises, we inspire lasting action to protect wildlife and habitats.

We train local champions to spread awareness about tortoise conservation through schools, communities, and traditional networks, ensuring that wildlife protection becomes a shared community commitment.
