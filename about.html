Our Story

The Tortoise and People Project was founded from a passion to protect Ghana's threatened tortoises and their habitats. Our founder, Dr. Kwame Mensah Asare, recognized that conservation could not succeed through science alone. By working with communities, we are building a future where people and tortoises thrive together.

From protecting tortoises to restoring their habitats, our journey has always been driven by community action. We empower local guardians, promote conservation education, and build partnerships that create a future where people and wildlife thrive together.

Our Journey in Conservation

Our Approach

We believe that effective tortoise conservation begins with the people who share their landscapes with wildlife. Our approach combines community knowledge, scientific research, and conservation education to protect tortoises and their habitats. We work with local communities to restore degraded ecosystems, promote sustainable livelihoods, strengthen wildlife protection, and create lasting solutions where people and nature thrive together.

From Ghana's forests to savannahs, we are protecting tortoises and restoring habitats while empowering communities to create a future where wildlife and people flourish together.

Where We Work

We work across Ghana's forests, savannahs, and rural landscapes, partnering with local communities to protect tortoise habitats, restore degraded ecosystems, and promote sustainable conservation practices that benefit both people and wildlife.

Our Partners

We collaborate with a broad network of local communities, government agencies, international conservation organisations, and research institutions to protect Ghana's amphibians, reptiles, and tortoises.
