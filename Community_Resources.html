Communities Leading Conservation

We empower local communities to lead decisions that protect tortoises, restore habitats, and ensure conservation benefits both people and nature.

People Protecting Nature

Strong communities create lasting conservation by working together to safeguard tortoises, habitats, and future generations.

Conservation Through Partnership

By building trusted partnerships with local communities, we create sustainable solutions that protect tortoises and strengthen ecosystems.

The Tortoise and People Project works alongside local communities to build strong governance systems that protect tortoises, conserve natural habitats, and promote sustainable resource management.

We empower communities with the knowledge, leadership, and decision-making skills needed to manage their natural resources responsibly. By strengthening local institutions and encouraging inclusive participation, we help ensure that tortoise conservation is community-led, sustainable, and beneficial for both people and nature.
