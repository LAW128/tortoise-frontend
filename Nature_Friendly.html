Nature Friendly Livelihoods

Protecting Ghana's most endangered amphibians, reptiles, and tortoises through community-led conservation.

Protecting Ghana's
natural heritage

From the Atewa Forest to the arid savannahs, we safeguard critical habitats for species found nowhere else on Earth.

Community-led
conservation

Empowering local communities to become the guardians of their own natural resources and wildlife.

Conservation thrives when the people closest to nature are empowered to protect it — and benefit from it.

Our Nature-Friendly Livelihood programme creates sustainable economic opportunities that reduce dependence on activities harmful to wildlife. By supporting ecotourism, agroforestry, and skills training, we help communities build futures where both people and tortoises prosper.

Impact Highlight

A Walkway to
Economic Renewal

In the communities surrounding Ghana's critical tortoise habitats, we are witnessing a quiet economic transformation. Families who once relied on unsustainable resource extraction are now running ecotourism ventures, producing nature-friendly goods, and leading community savings groups that fund conservation activities.

The impact extends beyond income. With stable livelihoods, communities are investing in education, healthcare, and local infrastructure — all while becoming the most effective guardians of their natural heritage. This is the ripple effect of conservation done right.
